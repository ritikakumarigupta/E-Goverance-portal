About the Portal

Empowering India Through Digital Governance

SevaSetu (Bridge of Services) represents India's dedication to accessible, accountable and paperless public service delivery for over 1.4 billion citizens.

Our Mission

To provide every Indian citizen with a single-window digital gateway for obtaining vital civil certificates, statutory identity documents, and direct financial entitlements without visiting physical government offices or dealing with intermediaries.

Our Vision

To transform Indian public administration into an inclusive, transparent, paperless, and real-time digital governance ecosystem aligned with the national Digital India, PM Gati Shakti, and e-Kranti benchmarks.

Foundations of SevaSetu

Core Architecture & Principles

Four foundational pillars that guarantee integrity, efficiency, and trust in every digital citizen transaction.

1. Transparency & Zero Leakage

All applications generate immutable reference codes. Welfare benefits are transferred directly to bank accounts (DBT) with end-to-end auditability.

2. Universal Citizen Inclusion

Mobile-first interface, accessible text-size toggles, assistive technology compatibility, and multilingual availability for urban and rural citizens.

3. Guaranteed Service SLAs

Mandated timelines for birth certificates (3 days), income verification (7 days), and instant e-Aadhaar downloads under Right to Public Services Act.

4. Data Protection & Privacy

Strict adherence to the Digital Personal Data Protection (DPDP) Act 2023. User data is never sold or shared with non-authorized commercial entities.

Simplified Workflow

How SevaSetu Works

01

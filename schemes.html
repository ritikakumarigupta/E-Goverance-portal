Ministry of Agriculture & Farmers Welfare

PM Kisan

Pradhan Mantri Kisan Samman Nidhi provides continuous financial support to landholding farmers across India to procure agricultural inputs and manage domestic farm expenses.

₹6,000 / Year in 3 Direct DBT Installments
100% Central Sector Funding
Ministry of Health & Family Welfare

Ayushman Bharat

Pradhan Mantri Jan Arogya Yojana (PM-JAY) provides cashless secondary and tertiary hospitalization cover to vulnerable and low-income families nationwide.

₹5,00,000 Annual Health Assurance per Family
Cashless Treatment in 27,000+ Empanelled Hospitals
Ministry of Electronics & IT (MeitY)

Digital India

Transforming the nation into a digitally empowered society through digital infrastructure, government e-services on demand, and citizen digital empowerment.

Paperless Governance via DigiLocker & UPI
Rural High-Speed Optical Fiber Connectivity
Ministry of Housing & Urban Affairs

PM Awas Yojana

Pradhan Mantri Awas Yojana (PMAY Urban & Gramin) aims to provide pucca houses with clean drinking water, electricity and sanitation to all homeless poor families.

Up to ₹2.67 Lakhs Credit-Linked Interest Subsidy
Direct Beneficiary Construction Assistance
Ministry of Skill Development & Entrepreneurship

Skill India

Pradhan Mantri Kaushal Vikas Yojana (PMKVY) enables youth to take up industry-relevant vocational training that boosts employability and self-reliance.

Free Certified Vocational Training & Stipends
Recognition of Prior Learning (RPL) Certification
Ministry of Women & Child Development

Beti Bachao Beti Padhao

Citizen Knowledge Base

Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to common questions regarding online e-governance service application, certificates and scheme benefits.

SevaSetu is the unified national e-governance gateway of India. Any Indian citizen with a valid mobile number and identity proof (such as Aadhaar or Voter ID) can apply for civil certificates, revenue documents, and government welfare schemes online without paying any commission or visiting physical offices.

Whenever you submit an application, a unique Acknowledgement Reference ID (e.g. SS-2026-XXXXX) is generated instantly and sent to your registered mobile number via SMS. You can use this ID to track real-time progress or submit an inquiry through our Helpdesk.

Yes. All certificates issued through SevaSetu are digitally signed by jurisdictional government revenue and municipal authorities under the Information Technology Act 2000 and are 100% legally recognized for all university admissions, passport applications, and central/state employment.

Scheme financial benefits are routed directly through the Public Financial Management System (PFMS) into the beneficiary's Aadhaar-linked bank account without any middlemen or physical cash handling.

You can immediately log a correction request on the Contact Helpdesk page with your application reference ID, or call our 24x7 toll-free helpline at 1800-111-7382 before the application enters the revenue verification stage.
